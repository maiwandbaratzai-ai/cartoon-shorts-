In 1932, Australia's army went to war against birds, and lost.
Twenty thousand emus were eating the wheat farms of Western Australia.
So the army sent a major, two soldiers, and two machine guns.
But every time they aimed, the emus scattered in all directions.
They mounted a gun on a truck. The emus were faster than the truck.
After a week, they'd fired twenty-five hundred bullets, and the emus were still there.
The army pulled out. The emus won.
