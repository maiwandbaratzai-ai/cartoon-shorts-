In 1908, something flattened 80 million trees in Siberia.
Forty miles away, a trader felt like his shirt was on fire.
Then a blast threw him clean off his porch.
The explosion was hundreds of times stronger than Hiroshima.
But nobody went to look for nineteen years.
So what did the first expedition find?
A ring of flattened forest, every trunk pointing away from the center.
But at the center, the trees still stood. And there was no crater.
Because the space rock exploded miles above the trees.
