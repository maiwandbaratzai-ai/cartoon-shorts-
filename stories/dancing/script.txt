In 1518, a woman in Strasbourg started dancing and couldn't stop.
Frau Troffea danced in the street for almost a week.
Then her neighbors joined in. Soon, thirty-four people were dancing.
Within a month, there were about four hundred.
So the doctors made their diagnosis: "hot blood."
Their cure? Even more dancing.
The city cleared out halls, built a wooden stage, and hired musicians.
But the music only pulled in more dancers.
So the city did the opposite. It banned music, and dancing.
