In 1835, a New York newspaper reported bat-people living on the Moon.
The Sun claimed a famous astronomer spotted them through a giant telescope.
First came blue unicorns. Then beavers walking on two legs.
Then the stars: furry winged men with faces like orangutans.
New Yorkers bought it. The Sun's sales exploded.
But the astronomer, Sir John Herschel, had no idea.
So how did The Sun end the story?
The telescope's giant lens focused the sunlight and burned down the observatory.
And The Sun never took back the bat-people.
