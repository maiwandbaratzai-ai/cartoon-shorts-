In 1960, the Royal Air Force parachuted cats into Borneo.
It started with mosquitoes. Malaria was spreading through the villages.
So health workers sprayed the houses with the pesticide DDT.
The mosquitoes died. But so did the wasps that ate caterpillars.
So caterpillars munched the thatched roofs until they caved in.
Then it got worse. Lizards ate the poisoned bugs, and cats ate the lizards.
The cats died, and the rats moved in.
So the RAF packed live cats into crates and dropped them by parachute.
The mission's official name? Operation Cat Drop.
