A showman marched 21 elephants across the Brooklyn Bridge to test it.
Leading them: Jumbo, six tons, on a bridge everyone feared would collapse.
Why elephants? Everyone said they never step on anything wobbly.
So the herd lined up trunk to tail, the showman out front.
[hype] But halfway across, the cables started to hum.
Thousands on the riverbanks froze. Would it hold?
[beat] Jumbo stopped, looked down, and lifted one giant foot.
[slow] Then he stomped. Nothing moved.
So the bridge passed, and the showman got a week of free ads.
That's how you safety-test a bridge: elephants.
