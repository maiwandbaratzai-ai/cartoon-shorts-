Why are 500 men lifting a hotel with the guests still inside?
Upstairs, a man pours his tea. If one jack slips, six floors fall.
The whole city laughed at them. Lift a hotel? With screws?
So the boss blows a whistle, and every man gives his jack one turn.
The hotel rises the width of a hair. Then another hair. Then another.
[hype] But then the walls groan, and a crack shoots up the bricks!
People sprint out of the street. The man upstairs? Still pouring tea.
So why would anyone lift a hotel at all?
Because Chicago was sinking. The mud was so deep, wagons vanished in it.
One sign in the street said, in big letters: No bottom here.
So the city decided to raise everything, and build sewers underneath.
[beat] The whistle blows one last time.
[slow] The hotel stands six feet taller, and the tea never spilled.
And the guests? Nobody even bothered to tell them.
