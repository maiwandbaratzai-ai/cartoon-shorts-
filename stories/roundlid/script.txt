Greta rolled a giant iron coin down Main Street, dog on top.
You see, Bellport has 300 holes in its streets, and every one needs a lid.
Her rival Bruno laughed: "Round lids? What is this, a pizza shop?"
Bruno's square lids were cheaper, so the town bought all three hundred.
Then came three weeks. Week one: a worker lifted a lid. Clunk. It fell in.
Week two: twelve lids fell in. But Bruno shrugged: "Just fish them out!"
[hype] Week three: trucks bumped the lids, they flipped, and forty dropped in.
[beat] Then Main Street was nothing but holes and traffic cones.
Ever carried a table through a narrow door? Tilt it, and it slips through.
A square's diagonal is longer than its side, so a tilted square lid drops in.
But a circle is the same width every way. Greta's lids can never fall in.
Bruno begged: "Greta, can I buy your lids?"
[slow] She rolled him one. Her dog rode it like a pizza delivery.
