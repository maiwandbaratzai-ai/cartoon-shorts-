Gustav made square barrels. Moving one took four sailors and a song.
"Progress!" he said. Square ones stack neater. So why are real barrels fat in the middle?
But Port Puddle ships three hundred barrels a day. By noon, the sailors ran out of songs.
Coconut, the ship's monkey, only cared about one barrel: bananas. It was at the bottom.
So Gustav upgraded again: perfect tubes. "Progress!"
A tube rolls great. Straight ahead. Straight down the pier. Straight into the sea.
[hype] Forty barrels bobbed away, with Coconut surfing on the banana one.
So what is the fat middle for?
A fat barrel only touches the ground at its belly. One sailor can roll it, and spin it like a top.
The curve is an arch. Iron hoops squeeze the boards tight, so it can't burst.
[beat] Gustav brought the fat barrels back. One sailor per barrel.
[slow] The other three started a band. Coconut drums the banana barrel.
