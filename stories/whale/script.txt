In 1970, Oregon tried to get rid of a dead whale with half a ton of dynamite.
A forty-five-foot sperm whale had washed up on the beach in Florence, and it stank.
So the highway department made a plan: blow it into tiny pieces for the seagulls.
They packed twenty cases of dynamite around it.
A crowd gathered to watch.
Boom!
Then it started raining whale.
Blubber flew everywhere, and one chunk crushed a brand-new car a quarter mile away.
And most of the whale was still on the beach.
