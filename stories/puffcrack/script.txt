Otto's crackers puffed up like pillows. His mouse moved into one.
Otto had banned the little holes. "Holes are lazy!" So why do crackers have holes?
Day one, the crackers came out round as hamburger buns. Otto called them "fluffy."
Day two, they swelled like beach balls. One rolled out and chased the mailman.
Nibbles the mouse was furious. His cracker house used the holes as windows.
So Otto stacked bricks on the oven door. "Holes are lazy!"
[hype] At noon, the door blew open and shot four hundred crackers over Crumbleton, like popcorn.
So what are the holes for?
Dough is wet. In the oven, that water turns to steam, and steam wants out.
[beat] The holes are tiny chimneys. Steam escapes, so the cracker bakes flat and crisp.
Otto put the holes back. "Holes are lazy," he says. "So I gave them a job."
[slow] Nibbles got his windows back. The mailman still runs past the bakery.
