In 1973, a truck hit the only tree for 250 miles.
The Tree of Ténéré stood alone in the Sahara.
Its roots reached more than 100 feet down to find water.
So caravans steered by it, and maps marked it like a city.
Thousands of camels passed it, but none knocked it down.
Then a truck came down the old caravan trail.
So how do you hit the only tree in the desert?
Rumor says the driver was drunk. The trunk snapped.
Today its dead trunk sits in a museum.
Its replacement is metal. Let's see a truck try.
