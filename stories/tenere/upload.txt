Title: A Truck Hit the Only Tree for 250 Miles 🌳
Description: The Tree of Ténéré, a lone acacia in Niger's Sahara that caravans steered by, was knocked down by a truck in 1973; its trunk is now in the National Museum of Niger and a metal sculpture stands in its place. Sources: Smithsonian, Atlas Obscura.
