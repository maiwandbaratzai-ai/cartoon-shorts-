In 2007, the Swiss army invaded a country by accident.
170 soldiers set off on a night march through the Alps.
But it was pouring rain, and the border had no fence.
So they kept marching, a full mile into Liechtenstein.
They carried rifles, but not one bullet.
Then someone checked the map. Wrong country.
So they turned around and tiptoed back to Switzerland.
But here's the part nobody saw coming: Liechtenstein has no army.
Nobody there even noticed the invasion.
A spokeswoman shrugged off the Swiss army: "It has happened before."
