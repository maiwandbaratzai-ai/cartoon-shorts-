In nineteen forty-three, the US Army strapped tiny firebombs to live bats.
A thousand bats per bomb, each with napalm clipped to its chest.
A dentist's idea: drop them on Japan, where they'd hide in attics.
Somehow, President Roosevelt said yes.
So in May nineteen forty-three, they tested it at an airfield in New Mexico.
Then it got worse: some armed bats escaped.
They flew under a fuel tank, and hung upside down for a nap.
Then the timers ran out.
Whoosh. The bats burned down their own test range.
So who did the bats firebomb first? America.
