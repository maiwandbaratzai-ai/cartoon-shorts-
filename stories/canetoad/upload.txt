Title: 102 Toads Became 200 Million 🐸
Description: In 1935 Australia brought 102 cane toads from Hawaii to Gordonvale, Queensland, to eat cane beetles. They never fixed the beetle problem, spread across the country, and one 2.7 kg toad found in 2023 was nicknamed Toadzilla. Source: Wikipedia, "Cane toads in Australia"; Queensland rangers (2023).
