In 1935, Australia imported 102 toads to eat beetles.
Two years later, 62,000 baby toads were hopping through the sugarcane.
The plan: gobble up the cane beetles wrecking the crop.
But the beetles lived high up on the cane stalks.
And toads can't climb.
So the toads ate everything else instead.
Then it got worse: they're poisonous. Snakes and lizards that ate them died.
Today there are over 200 million. One found in 2023 weighed 2.7 kilos.
Rangers named her Toadzilla.
And the beetles those toads were hired to eat? Still there.
