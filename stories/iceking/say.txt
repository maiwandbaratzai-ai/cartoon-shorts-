Why did Frederic Tudor ship a frozen lake to the tropics?
His ship sails into Caribbean heat, packed with a hundred and thirty tons of ice.
If it melts, he loses every penny he owns.
Back in Boston, everyone laughed. Newspapers called it a slippery idea.
Then he lands, and the ice is already dripping off the dock.
Worse, nobody on the island has ever seen ice before.

Second, he gives bartenders free ice, so every drink comes ice cold.
