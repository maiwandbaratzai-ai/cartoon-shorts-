Title: Why Lighthouses Wear Stripes
Description: "Less is more!" designer Sir Reginald paints five quirky lighthouses plain white — and the whole coast gets lost, cheese, yacht and pigeon included. The invented chaos shows the real reason: by day, each lighthouse's paint pattern tells sailors which one it is. Which lighthouse would you live in: stripes, spiral, checkers, polka dots or the mustache? 👇
