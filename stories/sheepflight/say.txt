Why were history's first pilots a sheep, a duck, and a rooster?
Their basket rises under a giant balloon. Nobody knows if the sky will kill them.
Below, a hundred and thirty thousand people are watching, including the king.
Scientists warned that the air up high could be poison.
So the balloon gets filled with smoke from burning straw, wool, and old shoes.
[hype] But it shoots up so fast the crowd screams, and a gust rips the cloth!
Then it drifts away, two miles, and vanishes behind a forest.
So why send farm animals first?
The sheep breathes like us. The duck already flies high. The rooster? In between.
If all three come back fine, the sky is safe for humans.
[beat] Riders gallop into the forest, and find the basket.
[slow] The sheep is munching grass. The duck is fine. The rooster is grumpy: the sheep sat on him.
Two months later, humans flew. But the sheep went first.
