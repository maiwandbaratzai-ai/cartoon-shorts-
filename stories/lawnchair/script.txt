In 1982, a man flew a lawn chair nearly three miles up.
Larry Walters tied forty-two weather balloons to a patio chair.
His plan: float thirty feet above his backyard.
Then the rope snapped.
He rocketed to fifteen thousand feet, into the flight path of LAX.
Two airline pilots reported a man in a lawn chair.
He shot balloons with a pellet gun to come down,
and landed in power lines, blacking out a Long Beach neighborhood.
The FAA fined him fifteen hundred dollars.
His reason? "A man can't just sit around."
