Andrew Jackson threw a party for a 1,400-pound cheese.
10,000 people mobbed the White House and tore into it.
The cheese was four feet wide and two feet thick.
A New York dairy farmer made it as a gift.
So on Washington's birthday in 1837, Jackson served it to the public.
In two hours, the whole thing was gone.
But the smell? The smell stayed.
A reporter wrote the carpet was slippery with cheese.
The next president had to air out the rooms and repaint the walls.
Then he banned food at White House parties.
Who throws a cheese party, anyway?
