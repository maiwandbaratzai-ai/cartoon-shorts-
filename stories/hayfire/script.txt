Edna's haystacks kept catching fire. The main suspect was a sheep.
Three fires in three weeks, and every time, Pickles was right there, chewing.
The famous Inspector Pomfrey sniffed the sheep and announced, "Case closed!"
He locked Pickles in the chicken coop. The hens were not consulted.
Pip the farmhand raised her hand. "The hay was wet when we stacked it."
"Wet hay can't burn!" said Pomfrey, and stacked a fresh wet pile as bait.
[hype] By Tuesday it steamed like a kettle. By Wednesday it was toasting his sandwich.
On Thursday it burst into flames, with Pickles still locked in the coop.
So who lit it?
Nobody. Wet hay is a buffet for billions of tiny bacteria.
[beat] They eat, they make heat, and the tight stack traps it like a blanket.
It cooks itself hotter and hotter, until it lights itself.
Pomfrey arrested the bacteria. Pickles ate the evidence. Case closed.
