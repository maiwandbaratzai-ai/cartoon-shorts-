Why did poet Zhay-rar duh Nair-val walk a lobster through Paris?
The lobster is named Tee-bo, and his leash is a blue silk ribbon.
Ladies gasp, kids giggle, and a poodle barks at it from a bench.
Zhay-rar tips his hat and keeps on strolling, proud as a king.
Then Tee-bo snaps at a pigeon, and the whole garden screams!
[hype] A gentleman jumps backward, right into the fountain!
So a policeman marches over and asks: "Muh-syur, why a lobster?"
But it gets worse. Tee-bo sits down, and refuses to move.
A crowd circles them. Reporters scribble. Some people bet he's mad.
[beat] Then Zhay-rar finally answers.
"Why is a lobster sillier than a dog? It's calm, it's serious,
and it knows the secrets of the sea. And it never barks."
Right then, the poodle barks. Everybody turns and stares at the poodle.
[slow] And that's why a poet walks a lobster through Paris.
