In eighteen fifty-nine, America and Britain almost went to war over a pig.
On San Juan Island, both countries claimed the same land.
Then a British pig wandered into an American's potato patch.
So farmer Lyman Cutlar shot it.
He offered ten dollars. The British demanded a hundred.
So the U S Army sent sixty-four soldiers. Britain sent three warships.
Then it got worse: four hundred and sixty-one Americans dug in against two thousand British troops.
But the British admiral refused to fire.
"I will not involve two great nations in a war over a squabble about a pig."
The standoff lasted twelve years. The only casualty? The pig.
