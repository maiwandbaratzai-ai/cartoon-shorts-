In 2020, it snowed chocolate on a town in Switzerland.
People in Olten found brown flakes all over their cars.
It came from the Lindt chocolate factory right in town.
A cooling fan on the cocoa-roasting line had broken.
Then a strong wind grabbed the fine cocoa dust
and sprinkled it across the whole neighborhood.
Sidewalks and cars turned brown, like a giant mug of hot chocolate.
Lindt said the cocoa dust was completely harmless.
And it offered to pay for cleaning everyone's cars.
