Why did one hungry sailor eat a breakfast worth a whole house?
He bites into an onion with his fish, and a merchant starts screaming.
So the sailor grabs his plate and runs, still chewing.
The merchant chases him through the harbor, past ships and fish carts.
Sailors laugh. Who chases a man over half an onion?
But the merchant brings the whole street. A baker, a fishwife, even a dog.
[hype] They corner him at the end of the dock, and he swallows the last bite!
So what was so special about that onion?
That onion was a tulip bulb, and that winter, Holland went crazy for tulips.
One rare bulb could buy a house, a horse, and a year of bread.
People traded whole farms for flowers they had never even seen.
[beat] So the sailor had eaten the priciest breakfast in town.
[slow] But a few weeks later, tulip prices crashed overnight.
He ate a house that was about to be worth an onion.
