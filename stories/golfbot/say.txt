Robot groundskeeper B twelve sanded every golf ball at Mossy Pines perfectly smooth.
At noon: the Club Championship. First prize: a golden toaster.
B twelve thought the dimples were dents. It buffed each ball until it reflected the clouds.
Then Margaret "The Hurricane" Pratt teed off with her famous drive.
Her ball flew half as far, plopped into the pond, and a duck kept it.
Next golfer: same. The third golfer hit the snack cart.
B twelve's fix: polish them shinier. The next ball barely cleared the golfer's shoe.
[hype] By three o'clock, the pond held ninety-two balls, and the duck was sitting on them like eggs.
So why does a smooth ball quit early?
It drags a big, messy whirlpool of air behind it, like a parachute.
Dimples make the air hug the ball, so the whirlpool shrinks. The ball flies twice as far.
[beat] B twelve learned fast. Now it adds dimples to everything.
[slow] Including the golden toaster. Toast now flies twice as far.
