In 1876, it rained raw meat on a Kentucky farm.
Mrs. Crouch was making soap outside. The sky was perfectly clear.
Then red flakes started slapping the ground around her.
Some were four inches wide. They covered a whole football field.
So two brave men tasted it. Their verdict: mutton, maybe venison.
Then it got worse: scientists tested it.
The meat was lung tissue, most likely thrown up by vultures.
Scared vultures vomit so they can fly away lighter.
So the meat those men tasted? Vulture vomit.
