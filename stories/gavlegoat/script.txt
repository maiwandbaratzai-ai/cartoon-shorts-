Every Christmas, a Swedish town builds a 13-meter straw goat.
Most years, it ends up as a giant flaming torch.
The first one, in 1966, burned on New Year's Eve.
So Gävle got serious: guards, fences, even webcams.
But the goat kept burning.
And it gets weirder: in 2001, an American tourist torched it.
He thought it was a legal tradition. He got 18 days in jail.
In 2005, people dressed as Santa and a gingerbread man shot it with a flaming arrow.
So far, 43 goats have been burned or wrecked.
So this Christmas, the town will build another goat.
