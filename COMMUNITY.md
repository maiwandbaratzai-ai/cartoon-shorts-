# COMMUNITY.md: keep every video inside YouTube & TikTok rules (read every run)

Added 2026-11-16 at Maiwand's request. Every video must pass the checklist at the bottom **before rendering**.
A strike, an age-restriction or "limited ads" kills reach and money, so when in doubt, leave it out.

## 1. Community Guidelines (removal / strikes)
- **Violence & gore:** cartoon slapstick is fine (bonks, falls, getting soaked, stars around the head). No blood,
  wounds, dead bodies, torture, weapons aimed at people, executions or realistic killing, even in cartoon form.
  Disasters and wars: show the event (smoke, a cracked dam, a toppled statue), never victims.
- **Dangerous acts:** never show or explain something kids could copy and get hurt by (fire tricks, explosives,
  stunts, swallowing things, challenges, weapons, drugs). Disasters in the story are fine as long as they're not a how-to.
- **Child safety:** no children in danger, distress or embarrassing situations as a joke; kid characters only in
  wholesome roles. Nothing sexual or suggestive, ever, with any character.
- **Hate & harassment:** no jokes about race, religion, nationality, gender, disability, body type etc.;
  no stereotyped accents or costumes; no mocking real private people.
- **Misinformation:** every video is now fiction (Maiwand 2026-11-21): invented characters only, and never labelled "true", never about current events, elections,
  health/medicine or real disasters with victims (SCRIPTS.md §6b). No fake medical claims.
- **Real people:** no invented scandals, crimes or quotes pinned on real named people; no politicians as jokes.
- **Spam / deceptive practices:** title and thumbnail must match what's in the video (no bait we don't pay off);
  no fake "part 1" with no part 2; hashtags ≤ 5 and relevant.
- **Repetitive / mass-produced content** (the YouTube Partner Program "inauthentic content" rule): every video needs a new
  story, a new set, new shots and its own script. Never upload the same video to both channels or re-upload old ones.
  Vary music, openings and titles; avoid template titles like "The X that Y" every day.
- **Copyright:** only our own synthesized music, sounds, voices and drawings. No real logos, brands, songs, movie or
  game characters, or real people's photos. Invented brand and place names on signs.
- **Animals:** cartoon animals can be silly, but never shown being hurt, abused or killed.

## 2. Advertiser-friendly (keeps full ads, "green $")
- No swearing in the script, captions, title or thumbnail (not even bleeped). "Heck", "dang" are fine.
- No shocking or disgusting close-ups (vomit, injuries, gross bodily stuff). Light "eww" humour only.
- Sensitive topics (wars, terror, tragedies with deaths, recent disasters) are best avoided entirely; if a historical
  event is covered, keep it respectful and non-graphic and never make the victims the joke.
- No drugs, alcohol abuse, smoking, gambling or weapons as the focus. A cartoon pirate's sword is OK; firing at people is not.
- Thumbnails/titles: no violence, no gore, nothing sexual, no misleading shock.

## 3. AI / synthetic content label
Our videos are clearly cartoon animation, so YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" label is NOT needed for them,
because clearly unrealistic content is exempt. If a video ever shows a realistic-looking real person, place or event
that didn't happen, tick "Yes" for altered/synthetic content when uploading. Never make realistic fakes of real people.

## 4. Audience setting
Our channel audience is "No, not made for kids" (general audience). Keep content suitable for everyone 13+, so nothing in
§1–2 should ever be a problem.

## 5. TikTok extras
Same rules as above; TikTok also restricts: dangerous challenges, content that looks like it targets under-13s,
graphic content, and unlabeled realistic AI. Original content only. Creator Rewards needs original, 1 min+ videos.
